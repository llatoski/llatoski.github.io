About

Physics for AI training

I am a physicist trained in statistical mechanics and complex systems, and since May 2025 I have worked as a domain expert on the training and evaluation of frontier AI models. The tasks are research-grade problems in physics, chemistry and mathematics. I write and solve them, trace model reasoning back to the step where it fails, and verify generated code against golden answers.

At Mercor I also lead the review of tasks written by other experts, and a team producing a video question-and-answer set. The work draws on five years of computational research in C, C++ and Python, and on three peer-reviewed papers. I am completing my PhD at UFRGS, with the defense scheduled for October 2026.

Background

Research background

My research concerns nonequilibrium statistical mechanics, and in particular the effect of memory on ordering processes. We model it with large-scale Monte Carlo simulations written in C and C++ and with master equations solved analytically. The full record of publications, talks and schools is on my academic page.
